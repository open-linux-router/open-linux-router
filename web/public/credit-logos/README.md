# Credit project artwork

Bundled locally so the credits page does not request third-party images at
runtime. Brand marks identify their respective projects; they do not imply
endorsement.

Files that carry a wordmark are cropped only by changing the `viewBox` to the
symbol at the left. No artwork was redrawn or recoloured.

- Linux: [`linux/default.svg`](https://github.com/GLINCKER/thesvg/blob/main/public/icons/linux/default.svg)
  from [theSVG](https://thesvg.org/icon/linux) (MIT). Tux artwork by Larry Ewing.
- IEEE: [`ieee/default.svg`](https://github.com/GLINCKER/thesvg/blob/main/public/icons/ieee/default.svg)
  from [theSVG](https://thesvg.org/icon/ieee) (MIT); the IEEE mark remains IEEE's.
- SQLite: [`sqlite/default.svg`](https://github.com/GLINCKER/thesvg/blob/main/public/icons/sqlite/default.svg)
  from [theSVG](https://thesvg.org/icon/sqlite) (MIT), cropped to the feather.
- systemd: [File:Systemd-logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Systemd-logo.svg)
  on Wikimedia Commons (public domain, Tobias Bernard), cropped to the symbol.
- Unbound: [File:Unbound DNS resolver logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Unbound_DNS_resolver_logo.svg)
  on Wikimedia Commons (public domain, NLnet Labs), cropped to the hexagon.
- mitmproxy: [File:Mitmproxy logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mitmproxy_logo.svg)
  on Wikimedia Commons (MIT, Mitmproxy Project), cropped to the orbit.

The page also draws marks already bundled for their own features:
[WireGuard](../access-logos/README.md), [Caddy](../service-logos/README.md) and
[NextTrace](../tool-logos/README.md).

Trademarks remain with their owners.
