# Access project artwork

Bundled locally so router pages do not request third-party images at runtime.
Brand marks identify their respective projects; they do not imply endorsement.

- WireGuard, Tailscale, ZeroTier, Cloudflare: Simple Icons (`https://cdn.simpleicons.org/<slug>`), in their published brand colors. Simple Icons artwork is CC0; trademarks remain with their owners.
- NetBird: `https://netbird.io/_next/static/media/netbird-icon.167cd80b.svg`
- Headscale: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juanfont/headscale/main/docs/assets/logo/headscale3-dots.svg` (BSD-3-Clause project).
- Nebula: `https://nebula.defined.net/img/mark.svg` (MIT project).
- SSH uses OpenSSH's artwork: `https://www.openssh.com/images/openssh.gif`.

Other entries use existing Lucide SVG icons with their protocol names below.
Lucide is ISC licensed and supplied by the existing `lucide-react` dependency;
no launcher artwork is generated or drawn locally.
